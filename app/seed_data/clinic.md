# Smile Dental Clinic (demo business, fictional)

## Services and prices
A routine check-up and cleaning costs ₹800 and takes about 40 minutes.
Teeth whitening in the clinic costs ₹6,500 per session. Most patients need one or two sessions.
A free 15-minute consultation is available before whitening, braces or implants.
Tooth-coloured fillings start at ₹1,500 per tooth.
Root canal treatment starts at ₹6,000, plus the crown.
Clear aligners and braces are planned after a consultation. Pricing depends on the case.

## Timings
The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 18:00. It is closed on Sundays and public holidays.
For a dental emergency outside these hours, please call the emergency line listed on our website.

## Address
Smile Dental Clinic, 2nd floor, Galaxy Plaza, Sector 15, Gurugram. Parking is available in the basement.

## Payments and insurance
We accept UPI, cards and cash. We provide bills for insurance claims but do not offer direct cashless insurance.

## Cancellation policy
Please cancel or reschedule at least 4 hours before your appointment. There is no cancellation fee.
