# Skyline Homes (demo business, fictional)

## What we do
Skyline Homes helps people buy and rent apartments and independent floors in Gurugram and South Delhi.
We list 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments, builder floors and a few villas.

## Areas we cover
Golf Course Road, Sohna Road, Sector 56, Sector 57, DLF Phase 1 to 5, Saket and Greater Kailash.

## Typical prices
2 BHK apartments to rent on Sohna Road start around ₹30,000 per month.
3 BHK apartments to buy on Golf Course Road start around ₹2.5 crore.
Builder floors in South Delhi to rent start around ₹55,000 per month.
Prices change often, so our agent confirms the latest options on a call or site visit.

## Brokerage fee
For rentals, the brokerage fee is 15 days of rent, paid once the agreement is signed.
For purchases, the brokerage fee is 1% of the property price.

## Site visits
Site visits run Monday to Saturday between 10:00 and 18:00. An agent accompanies you. Visits are free.

## Documents for renting
You need a government photo ID, proof of employment or income, and two passport photos.
The landlord usually asks for a security deposit of two months' rent.
